WE ARE A SEED STAGE ACCELERATOR

What is AngelPad?

AngelPad is a seed-stage accelerator program based in NYC and San Francisco. Since 2010 we have launched more than 150 companies. Every 6 months we select about 15 teams from a huge pool of applicants (usually around 2000) to work with us.
While other accelerators have decided to “scale” and take on more and more companies or launch more and more cities, we have stayed true to our original goal: Find awesome companies with founders we like to work with and spend three very intense months with them. This focus has paid off, we have risen to be the #1 Accelerator in the U.S. (based on a real study from MIT/Brown University) and Strictly VC called us the “Anti-Y Combinator“.

During the program, we work on everything from finding product market fit, defining a target market to getting first validation for the company. We are also instrumental in helping companies prepare for fundraising and of course for investor meetings.

IN SHORT: EVERYTHING TO BE SUCCESSFUL

What we do at AngelPad

Let’s face it, building a company is hard, especially the first year when there are limited resources, no or little support, and frankly all you have is you (maybe a co-founder) and the belief that you can do great things. That’s when we come in – we are always the first investor in companies, but more importantly, we are there for you: in our offices, together with you, figuring stuff out.

1:1 Mentorship

Mentorship is at the core of AngelPad and we don’t believe in the “one-size-fits-all” advices that founders often get. At AngelPad Carine and Thomas work alongside the startup founders. We roll up our sleeves and figure stuff out – everything – together with the founders.

Funding

Starting a company costs money. We invest $120,000 in each company. Startups have also access to more than $300,000 in cloud credits from AWS, Google and Digital Ocean.

Community

Working, not just alongside us, but with the other founders of the cohort will become the foundation of your support system. Being a founder can be tough, few people understand what you go through – no matter if you struggle alone or are responsible for hundreds of people. We are there for you during and after the program and founders often form strong fellow entrepreneur ties that subside long after they exit AngelPad.

Investors

Getting funded is a key component of startups’ success and a core focus at AngelPad. We know most investors, we know what they invest in and what gets them excited. Investors know us and the quality of companies emerging from AngelPad. Being an AngelPad company opens many doors.

You'll be in good company

AngelPad startups can be found in almost any tech vertical: SaaS, Marketplaces, Core Technology, Advertising, API, Mobile, Healthcare, AI, Data, B2B, B2C – even Drones. They all have one thing in common:  they tackle big problems in big markets and aim to build very big companies. Most of them are based in San Francisco and New York, but many come from all over the US and beyond to join AngelPad.

THIS IS WHO WE ARE

We are Family - Literally

AngelPad is Thomas Korte and Carine Magescas, a husband-and-wife founder team, who met working at – what else – a Startup back in 2001 in San Francisco.

Carine founded an e-commerce Startup back in 2003, the MOMA New York was one of many clients. Before she led teams in Product Management and Marketing, Strategic Partnerships and Client Success at several pre-IPO startups in France and Silicon Valley. In 2017 Carine was nominated “Angel Investor of the Year” at TechCrunch’s 10th Annual Crunchies Awards.

Thomas joined Google’s Product Team when it was just 7 people. He was responsible for all the Google Product Launches in Europe dating back to 2002. He spent 7 years at Headquarters and was at the origin of Adwords, Google Maps and Google Shopping. Thomas is the author of two patents which are still a crucial part of Google search ranking and ads monetization products.

If you join AngelPad you will work with us, in our office, every day – but you will also meet some of the best people of Silicon Valley: Investors, Advisors, Founders – like them, you will become part of the fabric of Silicon Valley.
