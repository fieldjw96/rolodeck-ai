Free · Require Approval
Join to run our first-party events in the city you call home (SF, NY, Seattle, London, Paris, or Berlin). You'll own the event end-to-end: the concept, the logistics, and the web of speakers, judges, and sponsors. You won't be doing it alone - we'll back you with our oiled machine: distribution to our 140k+ community, warm connections to sponsors and judges, venue help, and the playbooks we've built running events across six cities. Over time, as you ramp up, you become the point of contact driving those relationships. This is for someone early in their journey, deeply social and high-agency, who already loves bringing people together and wants to do so on a much larger scale. You'll work directly with Sahar, one of the leading voices in AI, who has built and scaled four communities, and you'll get wired into the AI ecosystem fast: organizers, companies, marketers, and founders. We're just getting started and the ceiling is high. This can become a paid role for the right person.
